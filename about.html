About Me   

My name is Yoshua Hernandez, and I'm currently in the 12th grade. I enjoy making videos and taking pictures, and I'm a big fan of movies—especially horror. One of my all-time favorite horror films is the original Halloween from 1978.

Throughout my time at El Camino High School, I've gained experience with industry-standard programs like Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and the rest of the Adobe Creative Cloud. I am eager to apply these skills to larger-scale projects in marketing and advertising—a field I am genuinely passionate about. I plan to major in this subject in college and build a successful career in the industry.

I hope to build a career out of this, and I can't wait to see what the future holds! In my free time, I love playing video games, listening to music, and hanging out with friends. I'm also a big fan of ice cream and cookies—especially in the summer, which is when I eat the most ice cream. Unfortunately, I spent most of this past summer working all week and wished I could've spent more time with my friends, but at least I got to go see the Spider-Man movie!
